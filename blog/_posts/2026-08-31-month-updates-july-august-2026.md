## Solar eclipse of August 12, 2026

In August, there was [a solar eclipse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solar_eclipse_of_August_12,_2026). It was total through Iceland and Spain, and something like 91% total from the UK.

Many members of the hackspace took pictures or viewed the eclipse from Sheffield :] — here are some methods and pictures that we saw:

> The classic pinhole in a cereal box for me.

<figure class="gallery" markdown="1">
![eclipse_11.webp](/assets/blog/2026-08-31-month-updates-july-august-2026/eclipse_11.webp)
</figure>

I managed to hitchhike to Spain in time to see the eclipse:] … I was thinking of looking through a CD, but in the end found some eclipse glasses. I didn't take a picture of the total eclipse, but I did take a picture of the people who were looking at the eclipse during totality!

## Adam - custom Skadis tool board

> It took me a moment but most of the tools are now living on the new board. A few last ones are waiting for the new holders in the Laser room. As usual, the "let's use  existing models" mantra has failed and I've ended up designing parametric holders in OpenSCAD.
> 
> Resources:
> 
> * wiki page: <https://wiki.sheffieldhackspace.org.uk/public/projects#tool_board>
> * Skadis board generator: <https://cutmaker.co/generators/ikea_skadis>
> * Skadis board SVG template for the laser (fits the large acrylic boards available in the space): <https://github.com/sheffieldhackspace/skadis/blob/main/svg/590x390.svg>
> * Modified Skadis universal hook (snug fit to match the acrylic board thickness): <https://github.com/sheffieldhackspace/skadis/blob/main/stl/Universal%20Hook.stl>
> * Various STL-s currently used on the board:  <https://github.com/sheffieldhackspace/skadis/tree/main/stl>
> * Source code for the Skadis Screwdriver Shelf Generator: <https://github.com/dredzik/models-skadis/blob/main/src/Skadis%20Shelf.scad>
> * Source code for the Skadis Tool Holder Generator: <https://github.com/dredzik/models-skadis/blob/main/src/Skadis%20Holder.scad>

## alifeee - Embroidered EMF Camp Patch

I saw that EMF Camp were selling pin badges, and they had some pictures of them online. I didn't buy them last time in 2024, but I was sitting next to an embroidery machine, so I thought I'd see if I could make the 2024 version.

I copied the design (via autotracing and manual edits) into Inkscape, and set it up for an embroidery run.

## Felipe - 1970s computer and emulation drivers

> I am building a 1970s computer in my kitchen 😉
>
> More info at: <https://forum.fiozera.com.br/t/historical-preservation-of-the-patinho-feio-computer-usp-1971/94>
> 
> I have been studying the history and the design of this computer for years! But more recently I actually bough chips and started manufacturing PCBs to rebuild the real thing. The server rack is still pretty much empty. It is fine to show the photo, but there are other complementary images that I think are interesting to show as well, that you can find at <https://forum.fiozera.com.br/t/resgate-historico-do-computador-patinho-feio-usp-1971/58>
> 
> I have also been writing MAME emulation drivers for Technics musical keyboards: <https://github.com/mamedev/mame/pull/15878>
> 
> with a cool YouTube video here: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tM5xOidLLn4>
